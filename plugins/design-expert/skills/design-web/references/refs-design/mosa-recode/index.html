FAQ

Questions answered, clarity delivered.

Everything worth knowing before you begin. Still looking for something? Reach out.

What exactly does this AI assistant do?

It is a quiet companion built to help you write, code, research, and hold your focus. Think of it as a steady collaborator that turns prompts into clear, usable results without the friction of older tools.

How is it different from other AI tools?

Most AI tools feel like software you operate. This one behaves like a guide that anticipates what comes next. The interface steps back so the work, not the tool, holds your attention.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan opens with a 14-day trial and no card required. Explore all of it, and cancel at any point if the fit is not right.

How is my data handled and protected?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never train models on private conversations, and you keep full ownership of everything you make. Team plans add compliance and audit controls.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Of course. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard whenever you like. Changes land at the start of the next billing cycle, with nothing hidden in between.

Do you offer team or enterprise plans?

Yes. The Grove plan and our custom enterprise setups include shared workspaces, role-based access, dedicated support, and onboarding shaped around how your team already works.

What kind of support do you provide?

Free accounts get community support. Studio accounts get priority email support answered inside 24 hours. Grove customers get a named account manager and a custom SLA.
